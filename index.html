Welcome to Chinaway

China is one of the most exciting destinations in the world, but planning a trip can feel overwhelming for first-time visitors. Questions about visas, mobile payments, trains, internet access and language barriers often make travelling to China seem more difficult than it really is.

Chinaway was created to bridge that gap and help European travellers explore China independently, smoothly and with confidence.
